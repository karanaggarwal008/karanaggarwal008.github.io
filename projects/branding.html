BRANDING BEYOND BOOKS · October 2024–October 2025

Looking closely at brands

Research reports, consumer behaviour and the outreach work behind a student competition.

What a brand asks people to believe

At Branding Beyond Books, I worked on research into branding and consumer behaviour involving companies such as Coca-Cola, Starbucks, Costa Coffee, Third Wave Coffee and Zudio. I prepared reports for internal review and circulation and helped coordinate programmes.

My Unstop analysis examined its transition from Dare2Compete, referral incentives, community engagement, partnerships, influencer marketing and positioning. It was a concrete piece of analytical work rather than simply a research-team title.

Research and outreach

I also headed the ambassador programme for a marketing case competition, handled outreach and helped distribute content. Those responsibilities required a different kind of attention from analysing a brand: explaining the programme, reaching participants and keeping communication moving.

I received second place in the Branding Beyond Books Marketing Case Study Challenge. That competition result and my organisational responsibilities are separately recorded.

What I carry into other work

Studying branding connected consumer behaviour with the strategy questions I was exploring elsewhere. It also made me pay attention to how an organisation explains what it offers.

The ambassador work sits alongside that analysis. Both matter, but they produce different outputs: a reasoned report on one side, and the practical delivery of outreach on the other.

The Unstop analysis

My original presentation script, Unstop’s Growth: Marketing Secrets, examined the change from Dare2Compete to Unstop. I looked at referral incentives, community engagement, partnerships, influencer marketing, brand positioning and growth. That gave the branding work a specific analytical object rather than leaving it at general discussion about advertising.

Other research reports considered brands including Coca-Cola, Starbucks, Costa Coffee, Third Wave Coffee and Zudio. Separately, I managed outreach and headed the ambassador programme for a marketing case competition. The reports were analytical outputs; ambassador work involved people and programme delivery.
