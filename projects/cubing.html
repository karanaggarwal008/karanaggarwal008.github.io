COMMUNITY & CONTINUITY · School years–2026/27

Keeping a club going

Reviving the Cubing Club meant permissions, marketing and helping the next team take over.

The work that keeps an activity possible

My Cubing Club involvement began with data-entry volunteering for the DPS cubing competition. Later, after founder Suhani Sharma left for Harvard, the club needed practical help to regain momentum. A change of principal and the lack of a separate teacher-in-charge made continuity harder.

Aastha Gupta and I helped with permissions, marketing, coordination and operational improvements. We also coordinated with Suhani while she was in the United States.

Supporting the next people

Aastha was my junior, and Suhani and I mentored her. She and Pulkit Malik subsequently continued the operations. An awareness session formed part of the club’s activity.

I remained involved in supervision and mentorship after graduation. The contribution was to help an existing club continue, and to support people who could carry it forward.

What had interrupted continuity

Suhani had brought substantial energy to the club. After she left, the principal changed, and the club did not have a separate teacher-in-charge or a clear ladder of junior positions to sustain it. The revival therefore involved more than announcing another competition: permissions, coordination and responsibility needed attention.

Aastha and I worked on those practical issues, marketing and coordination with Suhani overseas. Suhani and I mentored Aastha, who was my junior. The first successful activity after the revival was an awareness session, with Aastha and Pulkit later continuing the operations.
