QUIZZING & COMPETITION · 2024–26

Before the quizmaster finished

Some results stayed on paper. One memory is answering with Tejas before the question was even over.

The shared moment

Tejas Singh and I won the Shemrock business quiz. The moment I remember most clearly is the pace: answering before the quizmaster had finished speaking. It was a shared experience of recognising the question and trusting the answer.

Not every event ended that way. At Banyan Tree, I participated but did not win. It still belongs in my competition history.

A broader competition record

At SBI Scholar Quiz 2025, I became a city winner and reached the national Top 16 among more than 4,200 participants. Other results include the SRCC National Financial Olympiad finals and winning Quiz A Preneur at NIT Durgapur.

I also placed first runner-up in Strategy Spotter at IIM Rohtak. Quizzes and case contests exercise related skills, but they are different experiences: one often rewards fast retrieval, while the other requires building and defending a recommendation.

Keeping the teams attached to the event

For Samviddhi 6.0, the team was Tejas and me. I participated in Samviddhi rather than organising it. Samviddhi 7.0 was a separate experience associated with Vrindarak.

I enjoy the different rhythm of these events: fast recognition in a quiz, a longer explanation in a case competition, and the shared energy of working with a teammate.

The different scales of competition

The SBI Scholar Quiz record includes a city win and a national Top 16 finish from more than 4,200 participants. The SRCC National Financial Olympiad result was a national-finalist place among the Top 50 from more than 3,000. My SRCC results also include All India Rank 10 in the National Mathematics Olympiad, with 663 participants, and Rank 51 in the National Economics Olympiad, with 2,480.

The result I remember most vividly in a team setting is still the Shemrock quiz with Tejas. The larger competitions describe the breadth of my participation, while that moment—recognising an answer before the question was finished—captures the energy of doing it with someone else.
