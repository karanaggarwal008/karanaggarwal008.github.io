EduElevate

A continuing space for student discussions and practical ideas about education.

A conversation about education

Raghav Batla and I co-founded EduElevate in October 2024. The initiative focuses on student discussions and practical improvements to education, and it remains active.

It sits close to questions I have explored through tutoring, school leadership and research: how people participate, what makes an opportunity usable, and whose perspective is missing from a discussion.

Keeping the collaboration clear

Working with Raghav gave these education conversations a continuing home. The initiative brings a student perspective to questions that also appear in my tutoring and school leadership.

My education-related work now takes several forms: helping an individual learner, organising activities across classrooms, discussing practical improvements through EduElevate, and examining participation rules through research.
