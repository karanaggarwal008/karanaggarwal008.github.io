STUDENT LEADERSHIP · April 2025–May 2026

Opening the first professional door

An opportunity is more useful when someone understands how to approach it.

More than forwarding a vacancy

As President of the Internship Committee at DPS Chandigarh, I drove the committee’s work: sourcing opportunities, helping students understand applications and supporting early professional presentation. Opportunities came through direct employer outreach, school connections and student applications.

I organised a résumé-making competition and worked individually with around seven or eight students on their LinkedIn profiles. I also oversaw a communication group with more than 180 members.

Making the next step less unfamiliar

A student approaching a first internship may need help explaining experience they already have. The résumé activity and profile mentoring were ways to make that work more concrete.

Confirmed offers came through a mix of routes: direct employer outreach, school opportunities and applications students pursued with support. My work was to make those routes easier to navigate.

Room for a sustainable pace

I also prepared activities around balancing life and stress during Mental Health Week 2025. Professional preparation sits inside a student’s wider life; it cannot be the only thing that counts.

I continued mentoring juniors after school, including conversations with Pulkit Malik and Vrindarak Bajaj about educational and professional decisions.

The routes through which offers emerged

More than 50 confirmed internship offers were associated with the committee’s work. They came through a mixture of direct company outreach, opportunities sourced through school and student applications supported by the committee. I was the principal person driving this work.

The offer count is different from a count of completed internships. Alongside access to opportunities, I organised résumé activities, individually helped around seven or eight students improve their LinkedIn profiles and maintained a communication group of more than 180 members. These were related pieces of the transition from interest to an application or offer.
