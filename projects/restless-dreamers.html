When the subjects started overlapping

Behavioural economics, branding, psychology and systems thinking in the same learning experience.

A programme across boundaries

Restless Dreamers Summer Program Cohort 2 brought together behavioural economics, microeconomics, branding, neuromarketing, organisational psychology and systems thinking. It included practical assignments, field visits, reflections and seminars.

I liked the overlap between the subjects. A psychology discussion could change how I looked at a business problem, while an economics idea could explain a marketing decision.

The people around the learning

Ayush Anand, Nandini Tyagi and Sanika Deshmukh were part of the experience. Ayush also provided advice and confidence as I considered HPAIR.

The programme sits alongside my university summer courses. Together, they gave me several different ways of approaching questions about behaviour and decisions.
