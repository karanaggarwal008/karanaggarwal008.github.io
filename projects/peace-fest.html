GLOBAL YOUTH PEACE FEST · 2022

An early international perspective

A sustainability workshop in Malaysia, before my later diplomacy and student-organising work.

Outside my usual classroom

In Class 9, I attended the 15th Global Youth Peace Fest in Malaysia as a Yuvsatta delegate. The programme included a workshop on world sustainability.

It was an early experience of international youth activity. The workshop and the wider setting had some influence on my later interest in taking part in—and eventually organising—similar spaces for young people.

A beginning, rather than a finished identity

I attended as a participant. Later, through IDA and Model UN, I took on organising and speaking responsibilities. The Peace Fest is part of the earlier context for that progression.

The link between the experiences is an interest that continued developing, rather than a claim that one workshop caused everything that followed.
