SAMRIDDHI NGO · September 2023–March 2024

Care also needs coordination

Internal records and a student-led dog-welfare effort in Chandigarh.

The internal side of volunteering

At Samriddhi NGO, I managed interdepartmental communication, forms, volunteer records, onboarding documentation and basic HR processes. The role involved making participation and responsibilities easier to track.

These were practical supporting tasks. They mattered because an organisation needs to know who has joined, who has participated and what communication needs to reach each team.

A response to mistreated dogs

The dog-welfare activity involved food distribution by approximately ten student volunteers after concerns about dogs being mistreated. It was largely student-led.

I supervised attendance and maintained participation records for certificates and letters of recommendation. It was a practical way of supporting the volunteers who came forward.

The records were part of my responsibility

The dog-welfare effort was a response to mistreatment and involved food distribution by about ten student volunteers. My part was supervising who attended and recording participation for certificates and letters of recommendation. I did that alongside the broader internal-affairs work on forms, onboarding and interdepartmental communication.

That contribution was practical and largely behind the scenes. It supported a student-led activity by keeping participation records clear and connecting them with the recognition volunteers would later receive.
