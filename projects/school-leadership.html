DPS CHANDIGARH · 2024–26

The work between the titles

A grievance survey, an alumni meet, editorial feedback and helping students prepare to speak.

Hearing concerns

As Vice President of the Grievance & Legal Literacy Cell, I surveyed approximately 25 students and took concerns to the Student Council Coordinator. The role required listening to issues and finding an appropriate way to communicate them.

My Student Council contributions also included school representation, auditorium volunteering and the 2025 alumni meet. At the alumni event, I helped with coordination and guest hospitality.

Supporting the next piece of work

On the Editorial Board, I mentored younger students and evaluated newspaper-making work involving more than 200 students. Through the Debate Society, I offered advisory support, interviewed students and helped with debate preparation.

These contributions were less visible than a headline event, but they were practical ways of helping another student improve a piece of work.

A route into entrepreneurship

I founded and led the school Entrepreneurship Cell, coordinating participation in E-Cell IIT Bombay’s National Entrepreneurship Challenge Junior programme. I also represented the school in the Wharton High School Data Science Competition.

The cell, Internship Committee, company and club work overlapped in the people I met, but each had a different purpose. The most useful description is what I actually did within each.

Different responsibilities in the same school

The Grievance & Legal Literacy Cell involved gathering concerns from about 25 students and communicating them to the Student Council Coordinator. The Editorial Board involved mentoring and evaluating newspaper-making work connected with more than 200 students. The Debate Society involved student interviews, preparation and advisory support.

At the alumni meet in 2025, my work was coordination and guest hospitality. Auditorium volunteering and school representation were further Student Council responsibilities. These were separate tasks, each requiring a different kind of attention; I do not compress them into a single claim that I led every event.
