SWARASHTRA · March–August 2024

Behind the posts and the programme

Technical affairs meant digital content, communication and support for a MUN event.

The event’s digital side

I worked on Swarashtra’s organising committee in Technical Affairs. My work included digital content, Instagram posts and reels, marketing ideas, event communication and coordination for its MUN-related activity.

It was a supporting role in getting the event and its messages in front of people. The visual material and communication were part of the programme’s practical preparation.

A record of contribution

I received a certificate of appreciation for my organising-committee contribution to Swarashtra MUN’s Lucknow edition, signed by August Kumar Tiwari, Additional District and Sessions Judge, and the event’s other signatories.

The experience fits with the operational work I was doing through IDA, and later through other student organisations: content and communication have to support the event itself.

Digital work tied to a specific event

From March to August 2024, I supported Technical Affairs through digital media, Instagram content, posts, reels and basic marketing initiatives. I also suggested ideas for outreach and engagement. The work belonged to the organising process for Swarashtra’s MUN activity, rather than a generic social-media role detached from an event.

The local certificate records my organising-committee contribution at the Lucknow edition. It is included with this story so that the named event, role and recognition can be viewed together.
