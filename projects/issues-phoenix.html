Issues to Insights & Phoenix

Research, content and coordination, followed by leading a new initiative.

Supporting several kinds of work

At Issues to Insights, I contributed content writing, basic research, social-media outreach and event coordination. The role also touched mental-health awareness and internal initiatives across teams.

It was a mix of communication and support work. The contribution is best described through those tasks rather than by assuming that an IT-team title meant I built every technical system.

Beginning Phoenix

I subsequently took on Phoenix as initiative lead under Issues to Insights. It is an early-stage initiative in my community work.

Taking responsibility at the beginning is a different task from joining an established team. The work starts with giving the initiative direction and helping people understand where they can contribute.

The wider role and the separate initiative

My Issues to Insights responsibilities included content writing, basic research, event planning and coordination, social-media posts and outreach, and internal initiatives across teams. The IT-team label therefore covered a broader supporting contribution than technical development alone.

Phoenix was a separate initiative under that organisation which I directed. In my later account, I described it as starting out. I present the leadership responsibility at that stage rather than attaching completed programme outcomes to it.
