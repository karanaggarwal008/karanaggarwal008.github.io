CODE & CREATIVE EXPERIMENTS · 2020 onwards

The early making-things years

Games, small websites and experiments from before my university years.

A record of learning by building

My GitHub account preserves early games, web pages and coding exercises. The repository names are sometimes as direct as the work itself: Newton’s Cradle, Origami, Tutorial Hub and Nutri Putri App.

These projects belong to my learning history. They show an earlier version of me testing what I could make with code, rather than a portfolio of commercial software products.

From small projects to product feedback

I also took part in WhiteHat Junior’s Product Thinker programme on its advisory board from September 2021 to January 2022. I highlighted usability and learning-experience issues, suggested features and offered feedback on product flow and engagement.

The questions are still familiar when I work on a newer project: what will someone do next, where might they get stuck, and does the idea help them accomplish something?

Giving feedback on the product I was learning through

From September 2021 to January 2022, I was part of WhiteHat Junior’s Product Thinker advisory programme. My contribution was structured feedback on usability, the learning experience, product flow and engagement. I suggested features and content improvements from the perspective of someone using the platform.

The programme recognised those contributions with vouchers and prizes worth ₹12,500. That experience sits alongside my early coding work: one involved building with technology, while the other involved explaining where an existing product could work better for a learner.
