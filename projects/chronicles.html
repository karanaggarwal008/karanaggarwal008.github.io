CHRONICLES OF SCIENTIA · March–December 2025

The work behind a student organisation

Hiring, records, meetings and an ambassador system—the operating layer behind the visible content.

Coordinating across departments

At Chronicles of Scientia, I coordinated more than 60 interns across human resources, marketing, science, psychology, law and other departments. My responsibilities included applicant interviews, recruitment workflows, HR records and internal meetings.

A student organisation still needs a clear route from someone expressing interest to someone knowing what work they are responsible for. That operational layer was the focus of my role.

Introducing an ambassador system

I introduced an ambassador system and helped with policies, coordination and conflict resolution. I also monitored whether teams were meeting and functioning regularly.

I describe my contribution as organisational and HR work. The organisation circulated work across several subjects, but that does not make every piece it produced my individual writing.

Recruitment did not end at the interview

The HR work covered applicant interviews, recruitment workflows, records, policies and internal meetings across a group of more than 60 interns. It also involved checking whether departments were meeting and functioning regularly and helping with coordination or conflict when needed.

I introduced the ambassador system. That is a specific organisational contribution alongside the ongoing HR responsibilities. The organisation’s science, psychology, law and other departmental publications remained the work of their respective contributors; my role was to support the organisation in which that work happened.
