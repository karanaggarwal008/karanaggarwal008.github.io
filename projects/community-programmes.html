FELLOWSHIPS & SERVICE · 2025–26

Learning beyond the syllabus

Adaptability, effective altruism, an ambassador community and making space for another question.

Skills that need practice

I completed McKinsey.org’s Forward Program, working through adaptability, resilience and communication. These topics became more concrete when a pitch, a schedule or a team plan did not behave as expected.

The certificate records programme completion. Applying the ideas is a continuing task, especially in work where a decision has to be explained to other people.

Communities beyond a course

I participated in the Duolingo English Test DETermined Ambassador programme and graduated from it. Meeting other participants was a meaningful part of the experience, alongside the programme recognition.

My effective-altruism interests included a Cornell summer fellowship and EAGxIndia involvement. I have also undertaken fundraising work for Jankalyan Multipurpose Educational Society and Taare Zameen Foundation. These experiences sit within a broader interest in how an intention to help becomes useful action.
