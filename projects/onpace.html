LAUNCHX / FLAGSHIP · Summer 2026

OnPace

Five teammates, three time zones, and a study-tool idea that reached paying customers.

A problem close to the team

During LaunchX’s Online Flagship programme, Allen Yu, Hakan Atahan Aydın, Yashi Sodha, Zhongyun Zhang and I worked on OnPace. We wanted to bring multiple study tools into one place. We knew the experience of switching between resources; the challenge was to make the alternative useful enough for someone to choose it.

Unlike the Innovation programme, this project required us to create our own venture. We had to make decisions as a team rather than work entirely within a company brief.

Coordinating across the clock

The team worked across three time zones. Coordination was a practical constraint on the project, alongside the product itself. Progress depended on keeping decisions and work moving when we were not all online together.

Over roughly six weeks, we generated approximately US$260 in revenue. The team ranked second on the programme leaderboard and received a Best Pitch Award. I credit those as team outcomes.

The point where an idea meets a buyer

There is a different kind of feedback in a payment. It does not settle every question about a venture, but it moves the conversation beyond whether the idea sounds useful. OnPace gave me an early experience of that transition.

It also gave me a clearer distinction between a pitch concept and a product with some commercial activity. Both have a place in this portfolio; they represent different stages of building.

What the six-week result actually contains

OnPace combined a proposed study-tool experience with the practical work of forming a venture during LaunchX. The five of us worked across three time zones, so the programme’s roughly six-week period also involved coordinating people with different hours available. The team’s approximately US$260 in revenue marked a small but concrete commercial result.

The second-place leaderboard finish and Best Pitch Award recognise different aspects of the programme outcome. The original recording makes the presentation available to watch, while the revenue figure describes the venture’s activity during the programme. Both belong to the team of Allen, Hakan, Yashi, Zhongyun and me.
