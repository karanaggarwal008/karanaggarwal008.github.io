A research design for IARCO

Testing the moderating roles of seller reputation and bidder competition in online auctions.

Publication stage & contribution

Research proposal

Individual proposal by Karan Aggarwal

A proposal with a defined question

My IARCO 2026 proposal asks to what extent strategic under-pricing is associated with final auction prices, and how seller reputation and bidder competition moderate the relationship. It builds from an interest also explored in the four-author SISTER project.

I was selected for IARCO’s final video-presentation stage, and the organisers acknowledged receipt of my final proposal and pitch in October 2026.

The official IARCO 2026 Junior results list me at rank 23 with a score of 147. The official pitch recording starts at 4:59:42.

The planned study

The proposal targets at least 600 publicly observable, recently completed eBay auctions. It specifies a sampling protocol by category, completion status and collection window, with one auction as each observation.

The planned variables include opening and final prices, category, duration, bidding activity, observable bidder participation and seller feedback measures. The primary low-opening-price definition is the bottom 25% within a category, with a bottom-10% robustness check.

Models and limits before the analysis

The planned analysis uses linked ordinary least squares models and interaction terms, with HC3 robust standard errors. The proposal also specifies logarithmic prices and category-specific checks.

Because sellers choose their prices, the design treats results as observational associations. A target sample is a plan, not a completed dataset or a finding.

From question to a collection protocol

The proposal specifies a minimum target of 600 recently completed public eBay auctions, with each auction as an observation. Opening and final prices, category, duration, bidding activity, observable bidder participation and seller feedback would be recorded within a defined collection window. The planned category-relative thresholds classify unusually low starting prices without equating unlike products.

The design includes linked OLS models, interaction terms and HC3 robust standard errors, alongside log-price and category-specific checks. The interaction terms address whether the opening-price relationship varies with seller reputation or bidder competition. This is the prospective design presented for IARCO, separate from the historical samples already used in the collaborative SISTER work.
