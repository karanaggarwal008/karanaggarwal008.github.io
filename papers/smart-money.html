Smart Money, Green Choices

The role of AI and quality education in shaping sustainable investment behaviour among Indian youth.

Publication stage & contribution

Peer-reviewed book chapter

Co-author with Utsav Singh

Where three interests meet

This chapter connects financial decision-making, artificial intelligence and education. The focus is sustainable investment behaviour among Indian youth: how the tools people use and the education available to them relate to the choices they make.

It sits alongside my wider interest in how institutions and information shape behaviour. Financial understanding is relevant to a school activity, an investment choice and the way a digital tool presents a decision.

Shared authorship

Utsav Singh and I co-authored the chapter. The output is a book chapter, separately identified from my journal article and from manuscripts circulated as preprints.

The chapter title is Smart Money, Green Choices: The Role of AI and Quality Education in Shaping Sustainable Investment Behaviour Among Indian Youth.

Publication record

The chapter has DOI 10.25215/9141002121.31. I list it as a peer-reviewed book chapter.

The publication is the appropriate place to read its complete evidence and discussion. This portfolio gives the question and authorship context without assigning numerical results that are not reproduced here.

The question across AI, education and investment

The chapter brings three subjects together: the information an investment tool makes available, the education a young investor brings to that information, and the sustainability considerations involved in the decision. I am interested in their relationship because a more capable tool does not eliminate the need to understand the choice it presents.

Utsav Singh and I share authorship. The chapter’s complete title identifies Indian youth as its focus, while its publication form is a book chapter. Its DOI provides a direct route to the published work and its full discussion. I distinguish that completed output from the later working manuscripts elsewhere in this portfolio.
